1. Quadratic Equations & Complex Numbers (1 - 50)

1. Standard form: ax² + bx + c = 0
2. Discriminant D = b² - 4ac
3. Roots: x = (-b ± √D) / 2a
4. Sum of roots α + β = -b/a
5. Product of roots αβ = c/a
6. Equation from roots: x² - (sum)x + product = 0
7. Real & distinct roots: D > 0
8. Real & equal roots: D = 0
9. Imaginary roots: D < 0
10. Remainder Theorem: P(k)
11. Factor Theorem: P(k)=0 ⇒ x-k is factor
12. i² = -1
13. Complex number: z = x + iy
14. Modulus: |z| = √(x² + y²)
15. Conjugate: z̄ = x - iy
16. Multiplicative inverse: z⁻¹ = z̄ / |z|²
17. Polar form: z = r(cos θ + i sin θ)
18. Euler form: z = re^(iθ)
19. Argument: arg(z) = tan⁻¹(y/x)
20. De Moivre's Theorem: (cosθ + i sinθ)ⁿ = cos(nθ) + i sin(nθ)
21. Cube roots of unity: 1, ω, ω²
22. 1 + ω + ω² = 0
23. ω³ = 1
24. Triangle inequality: |z₁ + z₂| ≤ |z₁| + |z₂|
25. Distance between complex numbers: |z₁ - z₂|
26-50. Advanced algebraic inequalities, polynomial bounds & roots properties

2. Progressions - AP, GP & HP (51 - 100)

51. AP nth term: an = a + (n - 1)d
52. AP Sum: Sn = n/2 [2a + (n-1)d]
53. AP Sum (first & last): Sn = n/2(a + l)
54. GP nth term: an = arn-1
55. GP Sum (r < 1): Sn = a(1 - rⁿ)/(1 - r)
56. GP Sum (r > 1): Sn = a(rⁿ - 1)/(r - 1)
57. Infinite GP Sum: S∞ = a / (1 - r)
58. Arithmetic Mean: A = (a + b)/2
59. Geometric Mean: G = √(ab)
60. Harmonic Mean: H = 2ab/(a + b)
61. Relation: G² = AM × HM
62. AM ≥ GM ≥ HM inequality
63. Sum of first n numbers: Σn = n(n+1)/2
64. Sum of squares: Σn² = n(n+1)(2n+1)/6
65. Sum of cubes: Σn³ = [n(n+1)/2]²
66-100. AGP, special series summations & recurrence relations

4. Permutations, Combinations & Binomial (161 - 200)

161. n! = n × (n-1)!
162. Permutation: ⁿPᵣ = n! / (n - r)!
